    What Weight Loss Maintenance Looks Like in Provo, UT

    What Weight Loss Maintenance Means in Provo

    Your body remembers its old weight. After a big loss, ghrelin tends to rise and resting energy use drops a little, so hunger comes back louder than it was before you started. Maintenance care is built around that biology, not around willpower. On semaglutide or tirzepatide, this usually means stepping down a dose level rather than quitting on a random Tuesday. Weigh-ins every week or two give your provider the trend line.

    Who Maintenance Care Is For

    Patients who join have usually lost at least 10 percent of their starting weight on a GLP-1 or through diet and exercise. Some hit goal and want less medication. Others already tried stopping on their own and saw the scale climb back up within a few months. Transfers count too. If you lost weight with another clinic, your provider continues from your current dose instead of starting over at the lowest one.

    Dosing and Tapering Schedules in Provo

    Tapers move one dose level at a time. A common pattern for Wegovy is stepping from 2.4 mg to 1.7 mg, holding for four to eight weeks, and only then deciding whether another step makes sense. Tirzepatide steps down the same way, for example from 15 mg to 10 mg, then 7.5 mg if weight holds. Others find a stable lower dose and simply stay on it long term. Nothing changes until your provider reviews the last few weigh-ins.

    What to Expect Week by Week

    Week one after a step-down feels normal. Semaglutide and tirzepatide both have half-lives close to a week, so appetite tends to return gradually across weeks two to four rather than overnight. Expect a pound or two of drift, which is often water and food volume. Your provider asks you to rate hunger on a 1 to 10 scale at each check-in. A steady climb over three or four weigh-ins usually means pausing the taper.

    Side Effects on Lower Doses in Provo

    Stomach effects ease on lower doses for most people. Some patients still get mild nausea or constipation the day after their shot. Rarely, GLP-1 medications are linked with gallbladder disease and pancreatitis, and fast weight loss itself raises gallstone risk, so severe upper belly pain that spreads to the back needs prompt in-person care. Rising hunger is expected as the dose comes down, and it is the main thing your provider watches. Report vomiting that lasts over a day.

    Eligibility and Screening

    Screening repeats at every check-in, not just once. People who are pregnant or planning pregnancy should not use GLP-1 medications, and neither should anyone with a personal or family history of medullary thyroid carcinoma or MEN2. A history of pancreatitis or severe gastroparesis means extra review before any dose decision. Your provider also asks about insulin or sulfonylureas, since changing a GLP-1 dose can shift blood sugar. Patients who are not candidates get a plan built on protein targets and weekly weigh-ins instead.

    How Maintenance Compares to Active Treatment in Provo

    Active treatment climbs. Maintenance looks for the floor. In the STEP 1 extension, people who stopped semaglutide 2.4 mg regained about two-thirds of the weight they had lost within a year, and in SURMOUNT-4 patients switched from tirzepatide to placebo regained roughly 14 percent of body weight while those who stayed on it kept losing. That is the main argument for a planned phase instead of quitting cold. The target is a weight range you agree on, held with the least medication that works for you.
