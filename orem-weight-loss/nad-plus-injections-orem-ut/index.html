    How NAD+ Works in Orem

    NAD+ is nicotinamide adenine dinucleotide. In every cell it carries electrons through the reactions that turn food into ATP, and it is consumed by sirtuins and PARPs, enzymes tied to DNA repair and stress responses. Older tissue tends to have less. A subcutaneous injection places NAD+ under the skin, where it is absorbed into the bloodstream without passing through the digestive tract, though how much reaches each tissue is still being studied.

    Who NAD+ Injections Are Usually For

    Typical patients want steadier energy, clearer thinking or better recovery. Some add NAD+ during GLP-1 weight loss to support how they feel while eating less. It is not a weight loss medication and does not treat any specific disease, and people who are pregnant, breastfeeding, have active cancer or significant liver or kidney disease are generally not candidates.

    NAD+ Dosing and Schedule in Orem

    Small shots, slow push. A common start is roughly 50 milligrams under the skin a few times weekly, with increases every week or two depending on how you feel. Use the belly or thigh with a fine needle. Pushing the plunger over about a minute cuts down on flushing.

    What to Expect in the First Month

    Week one is about technique. A wave of warmth, some queasiness or brief chest tightness right after the shot is common, and it typically passes in a few minutes. By the end of the first month, responders describe more even energy, and plenty of people feel no difference. Your provider adjusts the ramp at check-ins.

    NAD+ Injection Side Effects in Orem

    Flushing leads the list. Injection site redness, stinging and soreness, along with nausea, lightheadedness and headache, are also common. Too much too quickly can cause chest pressure, cramping or palpitations, which typically ease with a smaller dose and a slower push. Rare allergic reactions need emergency care.

    Eligibility and Screening

    The review starts with history and your supplement list. Expect questions on cancer, pregnancy, organ health, heart rhythm and whether you use NMN, NR or high-dose niacin. Labs are not always required. Upload recent labs if you have them.

    NAD+ Injections Compared to Other Options in Orem

    IV infusions are the usual comparison. An NAD+ drip delivers a larger amount in one sitting but can take two to four hours, and many people feel more nausea and cramping during the infusion. The nasal spray is needle-free. NMN and NR capsules work differently, as building blocks.
