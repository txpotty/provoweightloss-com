    How NAD+ Injection Therapy Works in Riverton, UT

    How NAD+ Works in Riverton

    NAD+ is nicotinamide adenine dinucleotide. Cells use it to move electrons while making ATP, and enzymes like sirtuins and PARPs burn through it while repairing DNA and handling stress. Levels decline with age. Injected under the skin, NAD+ skips digestion entirely, but researchers are still working out how much of it reaches muscle, brain and other tissue.

    Who NAD+ Injections Are Usually For

    Adults interested in energy, mental clarity, exercise recovery and healthy aging are the usual candidates. People on semaglutide or tirzepatide sometimes add it. NAD+ does not treat disease or cause weight loss, and pregnancy, breastfeeding, active cancer or serious liver or kidney disease usually rule it out.

    NAD+ Dosing and Schedule in Riverton

    Small shots, slow push. Many compounded protocols begin around 50 milligrams two or three times a week and rise gradually, often toward 100 milligrams or more, as tolerance builds. Belly and thigh are the usual sites. A slow injection makes a real difference.

    What to Expect in the First Month

    Week one is about technique. A wave of warmth, some queasiness or brief chest tightness right after the shot is common, and it typically passes in a few minutes. By the end of the first month, responders describe more even energy, and plenty of people feel no difference. Your provider adjusts the ramp at check-ins.

    NAD+ Injection Side Effects in Riverton

    Flushing leads the list. Other frequent ones are stinging at the site, nausea, headache and feeling lightheaded. Chest tightness, muscle cramps or a racing heart can happen if the dose is too high or pushed too fast, and these usually improve with a lower dose or slower injection. Rare allergic reactions need emergency care.

    Eligibility and Screening

    A provider reviews your history, medications and supplements first. You will be asked about pregnancy, cancer history, liver and kidney health, heart conditions and any NMN, nicotinamide riboside or niacin you already take. Labs are not always required. Upload recent labs if you have them.

    NAD+ Injections Compared to Other Options in Riverton

    IV infusions are the usual comparison. An NAD+ drip delivers a larger amount in one sitting but can take two to four hours, and many people feel more nausea and cramping during the infusion. NAD+ nasal spray avoids needles at a smaller dose. NMN and NR capsules work differently, as building blocks.
